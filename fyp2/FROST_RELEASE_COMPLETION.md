whole own-Library Insights. Analyst history lists only current active/unexpired
exact assignments with active verified owners. Revocation hides the reviewer’s
notes/title/history; saved owner feedback remains under existing ownership. Both
use three-item progressive disclosure. Insights shows only owned recordings and
their actual durable states/durations/seven UTC dates, including for Administrator.
It does not analyze granted/private-other media or measure clinical effectiveness.

real abortable API client and frozen player/analysis components. No third-party

the owner. Sharing common separation/playback/technical-analysis tools is suitable:
the role distinction is collaboration responsibility and controlled access, not
